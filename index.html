LESSON 01

Unsystematic vs Systematic Risk

Unsystematic risk is company-specific (management scandals, regulatory fines). Holding 12-20 non-correlated assets eliminates ~85% of this risk for free.

LESSON 02

The Cost of Cash Drag

Cash nominal balance does not fluctuate, but inflation at 5-7% compounded annually destroys purchasing power over 3+ year horizons.

LESSON 03

Loss Aversion Psychology

The pain of losing $1,000 is psychologically twice as intense as the pleasure of gaining $1,000. Beginners panic-sell at bottoms due to emotional exhaustion.

LESSON 04

Beta & Volatility Exposure

Beta measures covariance against the benchmark index. Stocks with Beta > 1.4 will amplify both bull runs and brutal drawdowns.
